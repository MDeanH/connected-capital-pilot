The flow, step by step

Intake → Review → Screening Results → Threshold Configuration / Review. Upload documents, verify the extracted fields, then choose Confirm & Score to preview the screening. Open Thresholds to review the rulebook or try draft changes, then return to Screening Results. Use Record Decision and check for a recorded confirmation to save a decision.

Step 1

Drop in the documents

The offering memorandum (PDF, Word or text) and the pro forma (Excel or CSV). The workbook is parsed in your browser — only its extracted text is sent, never the file. The OM goes to extraction as the PDF itself (so citations can carry page numbers) or as text for Word and plain-text files.

Step 2

Extraction with receipts

AI pulls ~30 screening fields — costs, contingency, leverage, NOI, rents, entitlement, sponsor terms. Every stated value carries a confidence score and a citation to its source — document, page (for PDF OMs) and verbatim quote. It never guesses: a fact the documents don't state comes back empty and is flagged for you. Extraction usually takes a few minutes (up to ~15 for very large documents).

Step 3

The documents get cross-examined

The OM and the pro forma should tell the same story. Every disagreement — NOI, unit count, timeline, rents, capital structure — is listed with a severity tag. A deal's own paperwork contradicting itself is a diligence signal no spreadsheet catches.

Step 4

You verify, then it scores

Review what was extracted, correct anything, answer what the documents couldn't. Nothing is scored until you confirm.

Step 5

Gate 1: ten hard kills, eight watch flags

Character, entitlement, leverage, development spread, contingency, alignment, track record, exit dependence, construction protection, and a stress test. Any single hard kill fails the deal — strengths don't average away a fatal flaw. Flags accumulate into conditional approval. Every check shows its margin to the line, and thin passes are marked fragile.

Step 6

The economy moves the bar

Every week, eight economic signals — seven live feeds plus one analyst-entered PMI, covering recession risk, inflation, growth, credit spreads and the labor market — vote on the market regime, which sets a Green / Yellow / Red posture. Posture tightens or loosens the thresholds automatically: the same deal can clear the gate in a green week and fail it in a red one. Macro moves the bar; it never kills a deal by itself.

Step 7

Judgment, with a reason

Preview a threshold adjustment for this deal with a required written reason. Compare the adjusted verdict with the rulebook's original verdict. To save the adjustment, choose Record Decision and check that recording succeeded.

Step 8

The market reality check

The deal's returns stand next to what the S&P 500 actually returned over one, three and five years — and the loan rate the model assumes is checked against prevailing rates (SOFR plus a typical spread). Returns that lean on financing nobody offers get called out.

Step 9

Threshold Configuration / Review

Open Thresholds to review the rulebook or try changes in a local draft. Return to Screening Results to see their effect. Drafts are preview-only; saving a new rulebook version requires permission and a version note. It does not record a decision.

Step 10

Back-test your own history

Load past deals with their realized outcomes and see how today's rulebook would have called each one — right calls, missed winners, and whether the book ran above or below par. The gate earns trust by being scored against reality.

The idea that runs through it: decisions you can defend

Not a black-box score — a rulebook with receipts, applied under the economy you're actually in.

Hard kills don't average away

A deal with a fatal flaw fails, no matter how strong everything else looks. Averaging lets a great story bury a broken covenant; a gate doesn't.

Receipts, not vibes

Every stated number carries a confidence score and a citation to its source — document, page (for PDF OMs) and verbatim quote. If the paperwork contradicts itself, that contradiction is on the table before any scoring happens.

Missing data never passes

Extraction returns nothing rather than guessing, and an unanswered field forces human review — silence can't slip through the gate.

Judgment is welcome — with a reason

Overrides require a written reason, and the rulebook's original verdict stays visible alongside yours. A preview is not a saved decision. Successfully recorded decisions retain their inputs, config version, macro posture and adjustments for replay.

Macro moves the bar

Illustrative: the same coverage check under each weekly posture — the Thresholds screen shows your live rulebook.

The same deal can clear the gate in a green week and fail it in a red one — and the decision record says which posture judged it. Macro moves the bar; only the deal's own facts can kill it.

The rules the code enforces

Not policies in a document — invariants in the build. Read this before you trust any verdict.

Screening support, not investment advice

Gate 1 is a screen: it exists to kill clearly-broken deals early and route the rest to human diligence with the issues already on the table. It never commits capital.

Zero hardcoded thresholds

Every tunable lives in versioned config, editable on the Thresholds screen. If a threshold were a literal in the codebase, it would be a bug.

Recorded decisions are replayable

Successfully recorded decisions retain their inputs, config version, macro posture and adjustments for replay. Screening previews alone are not saved decisions.

Missing data never passes

Extraction returns nothing rather than guessing, and an unanswered field forces human review — silence can't slip through.

Your workbook stays in your browser

The pro forma workbook is parsed locally and only its text is sent; deal drafts live in your browser. The OM goes to extraction as the PDF itself (so citations carry page numbers) or as text for Word and plain-text files.

A red test suite blocks the deploy

Engine quality is enforced at build time by three golden suites — 62 checks covering the gate logic, the macro layer and engine parity. If any check fails, the site cannot ship.
